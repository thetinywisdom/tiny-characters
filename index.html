TINY CHARACTERS

Little pixel people and buildings from Tiny City Builder: a person is about 5 × 15 pixels. Move the one with the gold line (arrow keys / WASD, or tap where to go); tap anyone to make them talk. Walk round the town: tap a building to go to its door. The keys move you in the town, or in the park once you tap it. Open the catalogue: every person, animal and building, with filters and seeds.
